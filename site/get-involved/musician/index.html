Get Involved / Apply to Perform

Share your sound with the market.

We book musicians for every market day across our Austin-area venues. Acoustic sets, small duos, and family-friendly live performance fit our format best.

What to expect

Each market runs for four hours. Musicians typically play two or three 45 to 60-minute sets with short breaks. Our venues are outdoor or semi-covered, so weather plays a role.

We cover a performance honorarium plus a complimentary vendor-space setup if you sell music merchandise at your booth. We accept applications on a rolling basis and book the calendar one to two months ahead.

We listen to every application. Please share at least one recording or live clip.

We do our best to match. Flexible applicants tend to get booked first.

We respond to every application, usually within 7 business days.

Thank you. Your application is in. We will listen to your music and follow up by email within a week.
