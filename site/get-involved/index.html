Get Involved

There are three ways to stand with us.

Move Mountains is built by the community, for the community. Whether you want to share your music, lend your time, or join our team, we would love to hear from you.

Every market we put on is the work of dozens of hands. Musicians bring the soundtrack. Volunteers keep the gates open, the vendors supported, and the kids crafting. Our staff makes sure every detail is taken care of so artisans can do what they do best.

Pick the path that fits you. If you are a vendor looking to sell your work at the market, head over to our Vendors page instead.

Apply to Perform

Acoustic, folk, singer-songwriter, praise, or family-friendly live music. We book a rotating roster of Austin-area musicians at each of our market days.

Start application

Volunteer

Help us set up, run the kids craft corner, support vendors, or cheer on the community. Flexible shifts across every market day. No experience needed.

Sign up to help

Join the Team

Paid roles open a few times a year as we grow toward six venues. Event coordinators, vendor liaisons, marketing support, and more.
