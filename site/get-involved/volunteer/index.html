Get Involved / Volunteer

We run on community hands.

Volunteers make the market possible. Whether you have two hours or a whole market day, there is a spot where you can help.

Where you fit in

Shifts run from early morning setup through mid-afternoon teardown. You can pick a single role or mix and match across the day. All volunteers get a free market t-shirt after your first shift, snacks and water on site, and a standing invite to the vendor family.

No experience is needed. We train on the day for everything except kids programming, which we schedule with an adult lead.

Volunteers under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

We will email you a week before the next market with shift options that match your availability.

Thank you for stepping up. We will be in touch before the next market day with your shift options.
