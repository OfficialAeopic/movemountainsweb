# Move Mountains Artisan Market - Vendor Spotlight Caption Templates

**Usage:** Copy the template that fits the vendor's story, fill in the brackets, then adjust the tone to match any specific personality quirks or product details. These are starting points, not final copy. Every vendor spotlight should feel like it was written specifically about that person.

**Post types these work for:** Instagram Reel caption, Instagram Carousel caption, Facebook post, TikTok caption (shorten for TikTok, see note at bottom)

---

## Template 1 - The Origin Story

Best for: Vendors who started from nothing or have a compelling "how I started" story. Ceramicists, soap makers, candle pours, anyone with a visible process.

---

Before [Vendor Name] was selling out at the market every week, she was [making these in her kitchen / working a 9 to 5 / learning from her grandmother in the garage].

[BUSINESS NAME] is everything [he/she/they] built from scratch, and every [product] on that table proves it.

This Saturday, [Vendor Name] will be set up at [VENUE]. If you have not made it to her booth yet, May is your chance.

[CTA: Tag someone who would love this / Save this post so you remember the booth / Tell us what you are picking up]

Hashtags: #VendorSpotlight #ArtisanMarket #MeetTheMakers #LocalCreators #[PRODUCT_TYPE] #ShopLocal #ATXMarket #AustinMade #HandmadeATX #AustinArtisans #SupportLocal #SmallBusinessATX #MarketDay #LocalFinds #MoveMountainsMarket

Visual direction: Photo of the vendor at their booth or workspace. Product should be visible and prominent. If you can get a process shot, use it. Avoid blank-wall studio looks.

---

## Template 2 - The Product Hero

Best for: Vendors whose product is the story. Jewelry, food products, prints, wearables. Use when the visual carries the post and the vendor wants the product to lead.

---

[PRODUCT NAME] that actually [does the specific thing well / smells like something you want to live inside / fits like it was made for you].

[Vendor Name] of [BUSINESS NAME] makes every single [piece / batch / print] by hand, and it shows.

Find them at Move Mountains Market at [VENUE] on [DATE]. First come, first served. This booth does not have slow days.

[CTA: Which [colorway / scent / design] would you pick? Drop it below / Save this post for market day / Tag your person]

Hashtags: #VendorSpotlight #HandmadeGoods #[PRODUCT_TYPE] #ShopLocal #ATXMarket #ArtisanMarket #LocalVendors #AustinMade #SupportLocal #HandmadeATX #ShopSmall #MeetTheMakers #ArtisanGoods #ATXArtisans #MoveMountainsMarket

Visual direction: Tight macro product shot for the hero image. Texture, detail, craftsmanship. If it is jewelry, shoot it on a hand or neck, not flat. If it is food, show a cut or pour. Pull back one step to include the vendor in at least one slide if this is a carousel.

## Template 3 - The Community Angle

Best for: Vendors who are deeply local, multi-generational, neighborhood-based, or whose product connects to Austin/Manor culture specifically. Good for farmers, food vendors, culturally specific crafts.

---

[Vendor Name] grew up [in Manor / off 183 / in East Austin], and every [product] at [BUSINESS NAME] reflects that.

There is something different about buying from someone who is from here. [He/She/They] is not selling something generic. [He/She/They] is sharing [what she grew up eating / what her family always made / what this part of Texas looks like to her].

Booth [NUMBER] at [VENUE] this [DAY]. Come by and ask her about it. She will talk your ear off in the best way.

[CTA: What do you love most about shopping local? Drop it below / Share this with someone from Austin / Tag a neighbor]

Hashtags: #VendorSpotlight #ShopLocal #AustinMade #LocalCreators #ATXLocal #CommunityMarket #ArtisanMarket #ATXMarket #ManorTX #AustinTX #SupportLocal #NeighborhoodMarket #LocalEconomy #HandmadeATX #MoveMountainsMarket

Visual direction: Environmental context matters here. Shoot the vendor in front of a recognizable local backdrop if possible, or at their booth with neighborhood elements in the background. The story is "this person is from here."

---

## Template 4 - The Sell-Out Story

Best for: Vendors who consistently sell out, have a waitlist, or whose booth draws a line. Use this when social proof is the angle and you want to create FOMO before market day.

---

At the last market, [Vendor Name] sold out in [90 minutes / before noon / before we even finished setting up the next booth].

[BUSINESS NAME] brings [X amount] of [product] every time, and every time it is gone before most people find the booth.

If you want to get there first this time, market opens at [TIME] at [VENUE] on [DATE].

We are just saying.

[CTA: Set your alarm / Tag your shopping buddy / Drop a fire emoji if you are going to try to be first]

Hashtags: #VendorSpotlight #SoldOut #ArtisanMarket #ATXMarket #ShopLocal #GetTherEarly #LocalVendors #MarketFinds #HandmadeATX #AustinMade #SupportLocal #MeetTheMakers #MarketDay #FOMO #MoveMountainsMarket

Visual direction: If you have a photo of the booth line or the empty table at the end of the day, use it. "Sold out" is more powerful with visual evidence. Otherwise a close-up of their most popular product with a quantity note in the caption works.

---

## Template 5 - The New Face Introduction

Best for: Vendors joining the market for the first time. First-time vendors need a warm intro. This template reads as an invitation to come meet someone new rather than a promo.

---

Introducing [Vendor Name] of [BUSINESS NAME], joining Move Mountains Market for the first time this [month / season / year].

[He/She/They] makes [product description: specific, tactile, honest] and [one sentence about their process or what makes it different].

We are stoked to have them. Come say hi at [VENUE] on [DATE]. New vendor energy is always good energy.

[CTA: Drop a welcome in the comments / Who wants to be their first customer? / Save this so you remember the booth]

Hashtags: #NewVendor #VendorSpotlight #ArtisanMarket #MeetTheMakers #ShopLocal #ATXMarket #AustinMade #WelcomeToTheMarket #LocalCreators #HandmadeATX #SupportLocal #AustinArtisans #FirstMarket #LocalFinds #MoveMountainsMarket

Visual direction: For first-timers, a photo at their home workspace or a pre-market product flatlay works if you do not have market-day footage yet. If you can coordinate a quick visit before their first day, get a portrait with their product. Makes the introduction feel personal.

## TikTok Adaptation Notes

TikTok captions are secondary to the video hook. For TikTok vendor spotlights, use the template above as your script foundation, then cut the caption to 1 to 2 sentences max plus 4 to 5 hashtags.

Example TikTok caption adapted from Template 1:
"She started making these in her kitchen. Now she sells out before noon. Meet [Vendor Name] at our next market. #VendorSpotlight #ArtisanMarket #AustinTX #ShopLocal #ATXMarket"

The full story lives in the video, not the caption. Subtitles matter more than caption length on TikTok.

## Vendor Outreach Checklist (run before every spotlight post)

- [ ] Confirm vendor's preferred name and business name spelling
- [ ] Get their Instagram handle (to tag in the post)
- [ ] Get their TikTok handle if they have one
- [ ] Request 2 to 3 high-res product photos (or plan to shoot at the market)
- [ ] Ask one question about their origin or process for caption personalization
- [ ] Get approval on the caption draft before it posts (no surprises)
- [ ] Confirm they are attending the next market date before scheduling
