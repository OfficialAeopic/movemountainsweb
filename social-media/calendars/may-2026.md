# Move Mountains Artisan Market - May 2026 Content Calendar

**Client:** Move Mountains Artisan Market
**Market locations:** Easton Park, Goodnight Ranch, Whisper Valley (Austin/Manor TX)
**Platforms:** Instagram (~2,910 followers), Facebook (~1,800 followers), TikTok (23 followers, growth priority)
**Posting cadence:** Instagram 5x/week, TikTok 3x/week, Facebook 3x/week
**Calendar period:** May 1 - May 31, 2026
**Content pillars:** Promo 30% / Community + Behind-the-Scenes 30% / Educational 20% / Engagement 20%

---

## TikTok Growth Strategy Note

TikTok is the highest-leverage channel for this business right now. 23 followers means the algorithm has no anchor, so every post needs to earn reach from scratch via the For You Page. The play is market-day experiential content: authentic, slightly raw, camera-in-hand walkthroughs that feel like a friend dragging you through the market. Hook in the first 1.5 seconds every time. Text overlay captions matter because most viewers watch muted on first pass. Post within the 1-hour window before or after the market opens to ride search intent. Tag the neighborhoods (Easton Park, Goodnight Ranch, Whisper Valley) in captions, not just hashtags.

## Filming + Photography Checklist

### Recurring assets to capture every market day
- [ ] Wide venue shot on arrival (empty or near-empty)
- [ ] Setup timelapse (phone on tripod, 1 frame every 10 seconds for 90 minutes)
- [ ] Vendor portrait at each booth (ask permission first, always)
- [ ] Product macro close-ups at each booth (3 to 5 seconds per product, just hold still)
- [ ] Crowd energy shot (doors open, first shoppers, peak hour)
- [ ] Transaction moment (vendor handing over a purchase, smiling)
- [ ] One "walk with me" first-person continuous take (no editing needed for TikTok)
- [ ] End-of-day vendor packing up (shows the grind, great for Monday posts)

### TikTok-specific capture notes
- Always shoot vertical 9:16 when filming specifically for TikTok
- Natural audio is better than music overlays for the first 2 seconds (triggers algorithmic transcript)
- If you see a long line at a booth, film it. Lines are social proof and visual content gold.
- Vendor saying one genuine sentence directly to camera is worth more than a polished graphic
