# Vendor Monetization Upside - Working Hypothesis

**Date:** 2026-04-14
**Status:** HYPOTHESIS, not committed to Amanda
**Decision owners:** Sam (CEO), Justin (CMO), Theron (COO)

---

## Context

Move Mountains Artisan Market coordinates 55+ local artisan vendors across three monthly pop-up market venues in the Austin/Manor TX metro. Amanda (owner) signed at $100/month for a site-only deal on 2026-04-14. Justin flagged at signing: "we hope to make something off those vendors somehow." This document holds that hypothesis pending a real scoping conversation.

## Candidate Monetization Paths

### 1. Vendor directory paid listings
Each vendor gets a free baseline listing. Premium placement (featured on homepage, dedicated landing page, SEO-optimized) sold as an upsell either direct to vendors or as a revenue-share with Amanda.

**Risk:** Amanda may not want Aeopic charging her vendors directly. Could feel like Aeopic is stealing her customer relationships.

**Better model:** Aeopic charges Amanda a platform fee for vendor directory functionality, Amanda prices it to vendors however she wants.

### 2. White-label sites for vendors
Each vendor gets offered a simple Aeopic-built landing page or full site. Amanda gets referral commission (10-20%). Aeopic gets a new account.

**Math:** 55 vendors, 5% close rate = 2-3 new accounts per market. At $100-$300/mo each, that's $200-$900 MRR per market. Across 3 markets, $600-$2,700 MRR realistic in year one.

**Risk:** Vendors are often side-hustle or hobby-scale. Not everyone wants or needs a website. Pricing must be low enough to clear a side-hustle budget.

### 3. Vendor application / management portal
A paid module in the Aeopic Platform where Amanda can accept vendor applications, collect booth fees online, send booth assignments, and communicate with vendors. Replaces her current Google Forms hack.

**Price:** $150-$250/mo add-on, bumps her account to $250-$350/mo.

**Risk:** Tight coupling to Aeopic Platform roadmap. Not deliverable until Module 5 (SLA Builder) and Module 6 (Client Portal) are live.

### 4. Revenue share on market day sales (rejected)
Not pursuing. Amanda's market model is booth fees, not GMV commissions. Aeopic has no infrastructure for physical POS / sales tracking. Out of scope.

## Recommended Next Step

Kickoff call with Amanda (Hole 11). Present options 1, 2, and 3 as open questions, not commitments. Ask Amanda what she envisions for vendor upside before pitching any of the above. She may have a totally different model in mind, or none at all, in which case the $100/month is just the $100/month.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not build vendor directory pages into the site until the monetization model is agreed.
- Do not link to individual vendor websites from the Move Mountains site yet. Every outbound link is a missed monetization opportunity if option 2 is in play.
- Do not promise Amanda any specific vendor revenue number. Models above are Aeopic-internal planning math, not client-facing commitments.

## 2026-04-21 Update: GBP Access Secured (vendor-pipeline lever)

Amanda granted Aeopic manager access to the Move Mountains Google Business Profile on 2026-04-21. This is the first concrete lever on the vendor-pipeline hypothesis above.

**Why this matters for upside:**

- Every GBP post we publish tags the market venues and can be cross-linked to vendor profiles, building public association between Aeopic and vendors without cold outreach.
- Photo uploads of market days with vendor booths gives Aeopic a low-cost authenticated entry point into vendor trust (vendor sees their work promoted on a managed listing they do not pay for).
- Review response workflow gives Aeopic a credible reason to contact vendors directly ("we saw this review mentioned your booth, want us to amplify it on your own listing or page?") which opens door to option 2 (white-label vendor sites) without feeling like a cold pitch.

**Strategic framing:**

GBP management is priced inside the $100/mo site-only deal. This is below market rate for GBP retainer work (typical $150-$400/mo standalone). The gap is the implicit acquisition cost for the vendor pipeline. Do not re-bill GBP separately unless the vendor-pipeline hypothesis is explicitly abandoned.

**Immediate next moves:**

1. Confirm access tier in the GBP dashboard (manager vs owner vs site-manager).
2. Add GBP posting cadence to the weekly content calendar for MMM.
3. Photograph or collect vendor booth photos at the next market to have GBP-ready assets.
4. Start a "vendors spotlighted on GBP" tracker so we can measure which vendor relationships the GBP channel is warming up.
